Featured Works

Story that are told in two ways: through a physical exhibition and through a documentary film. Both ask the same question: how do marine and human life depend on each other?

We believe protecting Cambodia’s marine habitats begins with recognizing the ocean as our common home. From Kep’s seagrass meadows to Kampot’s lively waters, explore the projects where creativity, storytelling, and SDG 14 come together to safeguard our blue planet.

OUR EXHIBITION

Deep Sea Exhibition aims to teach young people that the outdoors is not a product that we can buy, but a world that we need to protect. By closing the education gap, this project is designed to turn "likes" into real action, helping others move past the aesthetics and connect with their wild root by highlighting the roles of the ocean as a vital living ecosystem.

OUR DOCUMENTARY

"BEYOND THE SURFACE"

In the shallow reaches of Kep and Kampot, traditional fishers find themselves at a breaking point as illegal trawlers and coastal development threaten their heritage—leading a defiant community to sink "anti-trawling" barriers into the seabed to reclaim their future.
