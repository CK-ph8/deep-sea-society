About Us

Let's dive in with us on getting to know nature's crew of the deep blue world. We learned how to protect our home, now let's get to know what protects our home.

Who are we

We are a group of 6 students from Limkokwing University in our last year of the Professional Design Bachelor degree consist of designers, photographers, and videographers passionate about sharing ideas through powerful visuals, and this is our Graduation Project.

MISSION

DEEP SEA SOCIETY is a project that is run by us with the mission to educate on the beauty and importance of our sea ecosystem. We started a documentary project about the lives of fishermen on the coast and now have a full-blown exhibition project in progress, targeting the next generation of kids and students to become avid supporters and protectors of our ocean.

OUR PURPOSE

We want people to treat the ocean like their own home, to reconnect with nature, and fall in love with the wild world we share. We're here to spread real knowledge and inspire action to protect it.

OUR VISION

To be a key educator in the future for kids and young adults who have an education gap about the ocean.
